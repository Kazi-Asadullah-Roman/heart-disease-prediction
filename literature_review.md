
# Literature Review
## Heart Disease Prediction Using Machine Learning

### 1. UCI Machine Learning Repository (Heart Disease Dataset)

**Source:** UCI Machine Learning Repository  
**Dataset:** Heart Disease (Cleveland Database)  
**Link:** https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/45/heart

The UCI Heart Disease dataset is a widely used dataset for studying heart disease classification. The Cleveland subset contains 303 instances and 13 predictive features, with the target variable representing the presence or absence of heart disease. The dataset provides clinical attributes such as age, sex, chest pain type, blood pressure, cholesterol, and maximum heart rate.

### 2. Detrano et al. (1989)

**Title:** International application of a new probability algorithm for the diagnosis of coronary artery disease.

**Journal:** The American Journal of Cardiology, 64(5), 304–310.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/0002-9149(89)90524-9

Detrano and colleagues developed a discriminant function to estimate the probability of coronary artery disease using clinical and non-invasive test results. Their model was developed using data from 303 patients at the Cleveland Clinic and evaluated on patient groups from other medical centres. The study also reported differences in prediction performance across the evaluated populations.

### 3. El-Hasnony et al. (2022)

**Title:** Multi-Label Active Learning-Based Machine Learning Model for Heart Disease Prediction.

**Journal:** Sensors, 22(3), 1184.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/s22031184

This study investigated active learning techniques for heart disease prediction using machine learning. The authors compared five selection strategies and evaluated their models using accuracy and F-score, with and without hyperparameter optimization. Their findings showed that different selection strategies can affect classification performance.

### 4. Banerjee and Paçal (2025)

**Title:** A systematic review of machine learning in heart disease prediction.

**Journal:** Turkish Journal of Biology, 49(5), 600–634.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.55730/1300-0152.2766

This systematic review examined 65 studies related to machine learning in heart disease prediction. It discussed the use of ensemble learning, deep learning, and explainable artificial intelligence in different prediction tasks. The review also highlighted important challenges, including limited public datasets, lack of external validation, and difficulties in interpreting complex models.

## Summary of Literature Review

The reviewed sources show that machine learning and statistical prediction methods have been widely explored for heart disease prediction. They also highlight the importance of selecting suitable features and evaluating models carefully. However, small datasets and limited external validation remain important challenges. Therefore, this project will compare two classification algorithms using the UCI Cleveland Heart Disease dataset.
